The approach

Strategy first. Professionals second. The owner — always.

Sequencing wins wars

Most entrepreneurs under attack run to a lawyer, then an accountant, then a bank — each sees a fragment, none owns the whole. Money burns, time burns, and the adversary sets the tempo.

I work the other way. First the full map: what is under threat, in which jurisdictions, on what timeline, with what pressure points. Then — and only then — the right professionals are engaged, in the right order, with defined tasks. They execute; the strategy is already made.

Every restructuring step is built with a documented, defensible logic — compliance by design, not by retrofit. Structures that survive scrutiny are structures built to be scrutinised.

The whole man

Resilience coaches teach from seminar rooms. I trained in the field — from communism through post-communism to London, keeping assets and family intact through seventeen years of pressure.

Behind that endurance stands decades of disciplined practice across traditions — Nepal, Tibet, Bukhara, the full Norbekov system — applied not as philosophy but as operational capacity: sleep, clarity and decision-making that hold up in year ten of a war, not just week one.

Body, mind, finances, risk: one system. A rescue plan is worthless if the owner breaks before it completes. This dimension is embedded in every engagement — it is not sold separately.
